# 0029. Comments on design screens and live design shares

- **Date:** 2026-10-21 (also 2026-10-26)
- **Status:** sign-in and team superseded by [share-with-any-email](share-with-any-email.md); who sees the reviewers by [mentions-in-share-comments](mentions-in-share-comments.md)

## Context

Feedback on a design is easier by pointing than by prose, and colleagues should review a design where it is made.

## Decision

Copied from `docs/SPEC.md`, where it was recorded as settled with Justin; the date of each part is in its text.

From `SPEC.md`, story 51:

> As a developer, I want to comment on the screens of a design chat, on a whole screen or on a point of it, and to send those comments to its agent when I decide, so that I give feedback by pointing rather than by prose; and I want to share a design chat's canvas live with named colleagues, signed in with their Google account, who comment the same way, so that a design is reviewed where it is made (decided with Justin 2026-10-21). A comment is a thread anchored on a screen or on a pin (a point of the screen, with the HTML element that was under it: selector and text), with replies, an author, and resolved or not; it lives in `comments.json` beside `canvas.json`, append-only, read by the agent as a file. In the canvas, a Comment mode (the toolbar's button, C): a click on a screen places a pin and opens a bubble (text, Send, Esc cancels), a click on a screen's label comments the whole screen; out of that mode a click selects as before. Full screen and Present have the same Comment mode (their bar's button, C), over their interactive screens: in it a cover takes the clicks (the pin's element is read from the screen as the user sees it there), a screen's label comments the whole screen, and Esc closes the bubble or thread, then leaves Comment mode, then leaves; out of it the screens stay interactive and their pins open their threads; a reviewer's page has it too (asked by a user 2026-10-26, decided with Justin 2026-10-26). A pin is a numbered disc: open, stale (its element is no longer found after the agent rewrote the screen; the point and the captured text stay), gone once resolved; hovering previews the thread, clicking opens it (Reply, Resolve, Reopen). A Comments panel over the right of the canvas (Esc closes) lists them by screen, Open or Resolved, with the count of new ones and one primary action, "Send N new to the agent": it sends the new comments to the agent as one message (screen, element, position, text; asking to address each and mark it resolved in `comments.json`), queued for after its turn when it works, and marks them sent; the composer's draft is left as it is, and a comment is sent at most once (asked by a user 2026-10-26, decided with Justin 2026-10-26; the comments used to be only written into the composer). The author edits or deletes a comment until it is sent; anyone resolves or reopens, the agent included (its system prompt tells it to set `resolved` on what it addressed; it writes nothing else there). "Keep these", beside Export PNG, sends "Keep these screens and remove the other sections: …" for the selection the same way. The author of a local comment is the Office's Google sign-in when there is one, else `git config user.name` and `user.email`. Sharing (the toolbar's Share): a share is the hosted, live copy of the canvas at `<share service>/s/<id>`, opened to reviewers named by e-mail (typed, the previous shares' addresses recalled), signed in with the Workspace's Google SSO (the same identity as the Office; a second, web, OAuth client of the same Workspace project); every design chat can be shared, explorations' included. The daemon pushes the design folder at each write of the agent and the comments over one connection to the share service, and takes the reviewers' comments back into `comments.json`; a reviewer sees the same canvas (pan, zoom, full screen and Present interactive, Comment mode, the threads), who is viewing, and "Live" or "Last update <ago>" when the Mac is asleep or offline (the share keeps its last state, comments sync at wake); no chat, no Export PNG, no sending to the agent. Share creates a new link each time; Stop sharing removes the hosted copy and kills the link (the comments stay local); archiving the workspace stops its shares. The owner also manages the reviewers from their own page ("Shared with" in its bar: the list with a cross each, a box to add, who is looking), with the same effect at once, and the app's dialog follows; a share is named for its reviewers: the design chat's title, or the workspace's when the chat kept its default name (decided 2026-10-22). A share can also be opened to the team ("Open to the team" in the Share dialog, `chauffeur share <chat> --team`, closed again with `--no-team`), named reviewers or not: every member of the Workspace then opens it and comments as a reviewer does, and finds it in the Office's Design window; nothing is open to the team unless its author ticks it (asked by a user 2026-10-27). A reviewer takes a design over ("Take over" on their page: the link, or `chauffeur import-design <workspace> <link>`; in the app, a workspace's chat menu → "Import a shared design…"): their daemon reads the share's files with their own Google token (the service lets a named reviewer's daemon read what their page reads), into a new design chat of their workspace, titled as the share, with its comments all unsent for their own agent; the share stays as it is, and the composer offers a first message saying where the design came from (decided 2026-10-22). Two agents never write one folder: a take-over is a copy. A reviewer's comment badges the design tab and the workspace row and notifies when the window is not in front; reviewers get no e-mail. The phone shows none of it. Out of scope: comments on sections, drawing tools, a share of an exploration's code.

From `SPEC.md`, Comments and shares:

> **Comments and shares (story 51, decided with Justin 2026-10-21).**
> - `comments.json` in the design folder: `{"comments": [{id, screen, pin?: {x, y, element?: {selector, text}}, author: {name, email}, at, text, reply_to?, resolved?: {by, at}, sent?: {at}}]}`, `x` and `y` in percent of the screen; entries are appended and edited by id, removed only by their author while unsent (a thread's replies with it, unless one was sent). The daemon is its only writer for the app and the share service (`design.comments`, `design.comment_add|edit|delete|resolve|reopen`, `design.comments_send`); the agent only sets `resolved` (the canvas reflects it at its next read). The element under a pin: the daemon appends a small script to a screen's HTML when the app frames it (a query flag on the screen's URL, never on the file, never for Export PNG or Open › Browser) that answers the app's postMessage with the element at a point (a CSS selector, its text, its box); a pin whose selector matches nothing is stale.
> - "Send N new to the agent" writes one message: one line per comment (the screen's path and label, the element's text or "whole screen", the position, the text, the replies), then "Address each one, then mark it resolved in comments.json."; `chauffeur comments <chat>` lists them; `chauffeur comment <chat> <screen> [--at x,y] [--reply-to <id>] <text>` adds one.
> - The share service: `crates/chauffeur-design-share`, Rust (axum, tokio-tungstenite), deployed as the Office is (a Docker container behind Caddy, `deploy/gcp.sh`), on the relay's VM (beside the relay and the cloud controller; decided with Justin 2026-10-21), reached at a hostname of its own (an A record on that VM's address; Caddy's Let's Encrypt certificate, beside the relay's IP certificate). It verifies Google ID tokens as the Office server does (audience of the desktop client from the daemon, of the web client from the page; `email_verified`; `hd` the Google Workspace domain the service is started with, `--domain`) and issues a reviewer's session, a signed cookie (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax, 12 hours, keyed at each start) made once their ID token was checked (decided 2026-10-21). Sandboxed screens and their relative resources use opaque read-only leases scoped to that share and reviewer (2026-10-26): `/v1/shares/{id}/render/{token}/{path}`, 15 minutes without renewal by the authenticated page, renewed every five minutes and on return from the background without changing a live URL. Each resource read checks the lease and current reviewer membership; a removed reviewer or stopped share is refused immediately. The lease authorizes no API calls or writes, carries no identity in its URL, is lost on restart and is never logged; responses keep no-store and no-referrer and allow credentialless CORS reads for modules and fonts. The iframe keeps its opaque origin; its subresources cannot rely on the page's SameSite cookie. Storage: SQLite on the VM's disk for shares, reviewers and comments, and the designs' files on the same disk under `/var/lib/chauffeur-design-share` (a design weighs a few hundred kilobytes; a nightly copy to a bucket comes with the VM's other backups), served by the service to signed-in reviewers of that share only, with the daemon's screen policy and the capture script on `?capture=1`. The daemon speaks to it over HTTPS (`POST /v1/shares`, `PATCH`, `DELETE`, `PUT /v1/shares/{id}/files/{path}`, a Google ID token as bearer) and one WebSocket per shared chat (`/v1/shares/{id}/daemon`: `Hello` with the token, `Comments` whole after each change, `Changed` after uploads; back: `Welcome`, a reviewer's `Comment` or `CommentRemoved`, `Viewers`, `Refused`); the page over `/v1/session`, `/v1/shares/{id}/snapshot` (the design laid out as the daemon lays it out, by the same code, each screen's URL on the page's origin), `/v1/shares/{id}/canvas` (again, after `Files`), the files, and `/v1/shares/{id}/reviewer` (`Snapshot`, `Files`, `Comment`, `CommentRemoved`, `Viewers`, `Live`, `Error` for one message refused, `Refused`; up: `Add`, `Edit`, `Delete`, `Resolve`, by the daemon's rules). Shares are 16 random bytes in the link; a stopped share's files are removed at once. Deployed by `crates/chauffeur-design-share/deploy/gcp.sh`: the archive to the relay's artifacts bucket, the relay VM's metadata and a reset; `relay/deploy/startup.sh` builds the image and runs the container (`127.0.0.1:8090`, the data folder a bind mount) and gives Caddy the name beside the relay's address. The daemon keeps one outbound WSS connection per shared chat (as it does for the Office; reconnects with backoff; `Hello` with a fresh ID token) for the comments, reloads and presence, and uploads files over HTTPS (one request per changed file, and `canvas.json` last). `design.share_start {chat, reviewers}`, `design.share_update`, `design.share_stop`, `design.share_status` (the link, the reviewers, who is viewing, connected or last pushed at). Settings: `[design_share] url` beside `[office]`, empty by default: sharing designs and workspaces, and importing a shared workspace, are off until it names a share service; the Google client of `[office]` is reused for the daemon's token.
> - The reviewer page: the app's canvas components in a second Vite build (`app/`, entry `share.html`) served by the service; signed out → "Sign in with Google" with the service's domain; not named → "This design was not shared with you. Ask <author's first name>."; stopped → "This share was stopped."; no screen → "No screens yet."; a banner with the design's title, Live or "Last update <ago>", and the viewers. Narrow screens: touch pan and zoom, the comment bubble full width.
> - The Office's Google sign-in becomes Chauffeur's: one "Sign in with Google" (the Office's, offered in the Share dialog too), used by the Office and by shares; its e-mail signs comments. The daemon side (`share.rs`): `design.share_state|share_start|share_update|share_stop` (`chauffeur share <chat> [--with a,b] [--team|--no-team] [--stop]`), `design.shared` (the colleagues' live shares open to the user, from the service's `GET /v1/shares`: those naming them, those open to the team, never their own nor who else is named; for the Office's Design window), a `design_shares` row per chat (share id, link, reviewers, open to the team; stopped at), the past reviewers in the store, and one link task per live share, resumed at the daemon's start: a fresh ID token (`dev:<email>` with `office.dev_email`), the Hello, the design's files uploaded (the folder scanned every two seconds: served kinds only, never `exports/`, dotfiles or `comments.json`, which goes whole over the socket when its time changes), reviewers' comments merged into `comments.json` by id (`merge_remote`, `remove_remote`), the state published as an ephemeral `design_share` chat event (connection, the share, viewers, `comments_rev` for the canvas to read the comments again, the last reviewer comment for a toast or a notification when the workspace is not in view). Deleting the chat or archiving the workspace stops its share. The app: a Share button in the canvas's toolbar (a dot for the connection, the viewers' count), the Share dialog (reviewers as chips, past ones offered, Open to the team, the link copied in a click, Live / Connecting / Offline, Update, Stop sharing; "Sign in with Google" first when signed out; a word when `design_share.url` is empty).

## Consequences

The share service (`crates/chauffeur-design-share`) runs on the relay's VM at a hostname of its own, behind a Google Workspace's SSO; the phone shows none of it.
